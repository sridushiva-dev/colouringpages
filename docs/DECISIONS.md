## Art Quality Bar

- Pure `#000000` lines on `#FFFFFF` background (no gray pixels)
- Closed, colorable regions; no broken outlines
- Original art only — no traced photos, logos, or branded characters
- One locked style reference per book; all pages must match

## Prompt Layout

- Top banner: 12% of page height — primary creative prompt
- Footer (optional): 5% — micro-challenge
- Art area: remaining center space
